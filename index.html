About

I am a Lecturer at Adelaide University Online. My PhD in Computer and Information Science (University of South Australia, 2024) developed causal inference methods for bioinformatics, with applications in cancer research. Before that I trained and worked as a mechatronic engineer in Colombia, where I designed biomedical and rehabilitation devices and hold awarded patents.

I have taught data analytics, machine learning, experimental design and programming for more than a decade in Australia and Colombia. I am interested in collaborations on causal machine learning, single-cell and cancer genomics, time-series foundation models, and learning analytics.

Code

Open-source implementations of the methods in my papers.

  • PTCR

    Pseudotemporal causality: identifies miRNA–mRNA interactions during biological processes, using samples ordered along a pseudotime.

    Paper: Bioinformatics 2021

  • PTC-pythonPython

    Python port of PTC that gives identical outputs to the R package. Depends only on numpy and pandas; install with pip.

    Paper: Bioinformatics 2021

  • DynamicCancerDriverR

    A causal approach for cancer driver discovery based on bio-pathological trajectories.

    Paper: Briefings in Functional Genomics 2022

  • DynamicCancerDriverKMR

    Identifies cooperative cancer driver genes with causal kinetic models over pseudotime, for bulk and single-cell data. Includes an in silico gene expression simulator.

    Paper: Royal Society Open Science 2025

  • AMCBGeneUtilsR

    Toolbox for gene expression pre-processing, shared by the Dynamic Cancer Driver packages.
