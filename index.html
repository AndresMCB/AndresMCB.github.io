Andres Cifuentes Bernal

Lecturer, Adelaide University Online

I work on causal inference and machine learning for processes that change over time. In my PhD at the University of South Australia I developed methods that order samples along a pseudotime and use that ordering to find the causal drivers of cancer progression. More recently I've been working on time-series foundation models, and I'm starting to look at learning analytics in online education.

Before moving to Australia I was a mechatronic engineer and lecturer in Colombia, working on rehabilitation devices, EMG signals and exoskeletons.

Research interests

Causal inference in cancer genomics

Finding genes and miRNA–mRNA interactions that drive cancer progression, using pseudotime to give cross-sectional data a temporal order.

Time-series foundation models

Making pretrained forecasting models work on difficult real-world data, such as rainfall records that are mostly zeros.

Learning analytics

Using data from online courses to understand student engagement and to improve course design.

Biomedical engineering (earlier work)

Wearable sensors, EMG signal processing, exoskeletons and assistive devices.
