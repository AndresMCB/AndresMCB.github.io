Andres Cifuentes Bernal

I'm a Lecturer at Adelaide University Online, where I teach data analytics and machine learning.

My research is on causal inference and machine learning for processes that change over time. In my PhD at the University of South Australia I developed methods that order samples along a pseudotime and use that ordering to find the causal drivers of cancer progression. More recently I've been working on time-series foundation models, and I'm starting to look at learning analytics in online education.

Before moving to Australia I was a mechatronic engineer and lecturer in Colombia, working on rehabilitation devices, EMG signals and exoskeletons.

Software

  • PTC (R) and PTC-python: pseudotemporal causality for miRNA-mRNA interactions. The Python version gives the same results and installs with pip.
  • DynamicCancerDriverKM (R): cooperative cancer drivers with causal kinetic models, plus a gene expression simulator.
  • DynamicCancerDriver (R): cancer driver discovery along bio-pathological trajectories.
  • AMCBGeneUtils (R): gene expression pre-processing utilities.
